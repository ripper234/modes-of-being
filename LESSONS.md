## The practice line is the "how", in the person's own words (2026-10-18)

On the daily summary image, the single practice line carries how and where the chosen modes will be expressed, in the person's own words. Never repeat the modes themselves as the practice line (for example "Authentic, breathing, compassionate." adds nothing). If the "how" for a chosen mode is unknown, ask the person for it and remember the answer for next time.

### Known practice mappings

- Breathing (2026-10-18): "Take two seconds to imagine the other's body reaction to what I say." (Ron described this as his breathing and empathy practice.)
